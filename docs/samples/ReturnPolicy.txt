1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy sets out the conditions under which Contoso Trading Services
accepts the return of goods sold to business customers, and the basis on
which refunds are calculated and paid. It applies to all products in the
Electronics, Office Furniture, Computer Accessories, Networking, Printers
and Scanners, Storage Devices, Software Licenses and Consumables
categories, across all four sales regions.

This policy covers commercial returns only. Faults arising after the
return window has closed are handled under document CTS-POL-007, Product
Warranty Terms.


2. RETURN WINDOW

2.1  The standard return window is 30 calendar days from the date of
     delivery. A return request raised after day 30 will be rejected
     automatically by the returns system.

2.2  For orders shipped to the Middle East region, the window is extended
     to 45 calendar days to allow for longer transit and customs clearance
     times.

2.3  Goods that arrive damaged must be reported within 5 business days of
     delivery, with photographic evidence, otherwise the claim is treated
     as a change-of-mind return under section 4.2.

2.4  The delivery date recorded in the order management system is the
     authoritative date for calculating the window. Customer-side receipt
     dates are not accepted as evidence.


3. ELIGIBILITY

3.1  Goods must be returned in their original packaging, complete with all
     accessories, manuals, cables and license cards.

3.2  The following items are NOT returnable under any circumstances:

     (a) Consumables where the sealed packaging has been opened, including
         toner cartridges, drum units and printing paper.
     (b) Software licenses where the activation key has been issued or
         redeemed.
     (c) Office furniture that has been built to a custom specification,
         including bespoke modular workstations and acoustic meeting pods.
     (d) Products marked as discontinued at the time of the return request.
     (e) Any product sold as clearance or ex-demonstration stock.

3.3  Partial returns are permitted. A customer may return part of the
     quantity on an order line without returning the whole line.


4. REFUND CALCULATION

4.1  Where the return reason is DAMAGED, WRONG_ITEM or NOT_AS_DESCRIBED,
     the customer receives a full refund of the line value, calculated as
     the returned quantity multiplied by the unit price actually charged,
     less any discount that was applied to that line. No restocking fee
     is charged.

4.2  Where the return reason is CHANGED_MIND, a restocking fee of 15 per
     cent of the line value is deducted from the refund. The customer
     therefore receives 85 per cent of the calculated line value.

4.3  Where the return reason is LATE_DELIVERY, the refund is issued in
     full and the freight charge on the original order is also credited.

4.4  Freight charges are not otherwise refundable.

4.5  Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Where the original
     payment was made by cheque, the refund is issued by bank transfer.


5. RETURN AUTHORISATION PROCESS

5.1  The customer raises a return request through their account executive
     or the customer portal, quoting the original order number.

5.2  Customer Operations issues a Return Material Authorisation number
     within 2 business days. Goods received without an RMA number
     referenced on the outer packaging will be refused at the dock.

5.3  Returned goods are inspected within 3 business days of receipt at
     the regional warehouse.

5.4  The return is then recorded with one of three statuses:

     Approved  - inspection passed, refund proceeds
     Pending   - awaiting further information from the customer
     Rejected  - the goods failed inspection or fell outside this policy

5.5  Refunds are paid within 7 business days of a return moving to
     Approved status.


6. RETURN SHIPPING COSTS

6.1  Contoso Trading Services pays return freight where the reason code is
     DAMAGED, WRONG_ITEM, NOT_AS_DESCRIBED or LATE_DELIVERY.

6.2  The customer pays return freight for CHANGED_MIND returns.

6.3  Returns of items weighing more than 30 kilograms, or of any item in
     the Office Furniture category, must be collected by our nominated
     carrier and must not be sent by the customer's own courier.


7. REASON CODES

The following reason codes are the only values accepted by the returns
system. Account executives must select the closest match; free-text
reasons are not recorded.

     DAMAGED           Goods physically damaged in transit or on arrival
     WRONG_ITEM        A product other than the one ordered was shipped
     NOT_AS_DESCRIBED  Specification differs from the published details
     LATE_DELIVERY     Delivered after the required date on the order
     CHANGED_MIND      Customer no longer requires the goods


8. ESCALATION

8.1  A rejected return may be escalated to the Regional Sales Manager for
     the customer's region within 10 business days of the rejection.

8.2  Disputes not resolved at regional level are referred to the VP Sales.
     The decision of the VP Sales is final.


9. RECORD KEEPING

All return records, including the reason code, quantity, refund amount and
final status, are retained for 7 years in line with the company's
financial record retention schedule.
